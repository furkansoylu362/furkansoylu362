## 👨‍💻 About Me

I am a **Computer Engineer** (Bilgisayar Mühendisi) and the **Founder & Lead Software Engineer** at [**Shifoo Soft**](https://shifoosoft.com). 

I specialize in architecting cross-platform mobile applications (**Flutter**, **React Native**) and engineering robust, scalable web platforms (**ASP.NET Core**, **Next.js**). My focus is on turning complex business workflows into seamless, high-performance, and user-centric digital products.

- 🚀 **Studio:** Founder of [Shifoo Soft](https://shifoosoft.com) — Engineering future-ready mobile apps and enterprise software.
- 💡 **Philosophy:** Offline-first architectures, maximum data privacy, and sub-millisecond response times.
- 🎓 **Background:** Computer Engineering graduate with deep experience in mobile, backend, and distributed systems.
